# FCA perimeter self-assessment (internal, to give the adviser)

Not legal advice. Facts to check with the adviser are marked **(verify)**.

## What the service does
| Feature | Position |
|---|---|
| Explains quote-form questions in general terms | Information |
| Guided check ranks "what is worth testing" with a general fit label | General information. Wording avoids "you should", recommendations and named products. Open question for adviser. |
| Test plan: user enters prices they obtained elsewhere; tool shows % change and noise | Calculation on the user's own data |
| Comparer: expected cost = price + claim chance x excess + missing cover | Calculation on the user's own data. Label is "Lowest expected cost". Adviser to confirm this is not a recommendation. |
| Community findings | Aggregate statistics, minimum 20 reports, no insurer or site ranking |
| Live quotes, quote journeys, buy buttons, referral links | **None** |
| Commission, affiliate links, paid placement | **None** |
| Names an insurer or comparison site as better or worse | **No.** The `site` field is collected but not published. |

## Why role clarity matters
The FCA's earlier review of price comparison websites reported that some consumers wrongly believed a comparison site had advised them on which policy to choose, and that sites did not always make their role clear. That is why every page footer, the terms, the guided check and the share panel state that Quote Guide is not authorised by the FCA, does not sell or arrange insurance, and does not recommend an insurer or policy.

## Regulatory background to confirm (verify)
- UK insurance distribution rules derive from the EU Insurance Distribution Directive. That directive's recitals exclude the mere provision of information, where the provider takes no further steps to help conclude a contract. Confirm how the UK Regulated Activities Order and FCA guidance (PERG 5) treat this today.
- Whether "advising on", "arranging" or "assisting in the administration of" a contract of insurance could apply to any feature above.
- Whether the guided check's fit label or ordering could be read as a personal recommendation.
- Financial promotion rules if we ever promote a specific product.

## Changes that would change the position (stop and take advice first)
- Any affiliate link, referral fee, sponsorship or paid placement.
- Any live quote, price feed, or "buy" or "get this quote" button.
- Naming or ranking insurers, comparison sites, or brokers.
- A browser add-on that fills forms or steers a purchase.
- Collecting data to pass to insurers or brokers.

## Marketing claims (verify)
"Free", "independent" and "no affiliate links" are statements to consumers. Keep them true. The Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024 covers misleading practices. Review claims at launch and whenever the model changes.

## Gender
Gender is not asked anywhere. UK insurers have not been allowed to price on gender since 2012, so asking would add nothing and could mislead.

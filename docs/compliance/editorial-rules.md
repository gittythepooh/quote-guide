# Editorial rules (keeps us on the information side of the line)

Enforced in part by `scripts/check-wording.mjs`.

**Never write:** "you should buy/choose/switch", "we recommend", "best insurer/policy/deal", "cheapest job title", "guaranteed", "you will save", anything that suggests false answers, or any ranking of insurers or comparison sites.

**Do write:** "in tests, adding a named driver changed quotes by about 15%", "for profiles like yours", "fit for your answers", "test this with an honest option", "must be true when you buy".

**Always show:** sample size and source for numbers; that results vary; that answers must be true; that we are not authorised by the FCA and do not sell or arrange insurance.

**Job titles:** only ever "another title that is genuinely accurate for you". Never suggest choosing a title for price alone.

**Gender:** not asked, not used.

**Before adding a feature, ask:** does it recommend, rank, link out for money, show live quotes, collect free text or new identifiers? If yes, stop and take advice.

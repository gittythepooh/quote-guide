# Job titles: what data exists

Checked 19 Oct 2026 (web search; unverified claims marked).

- **No public, downloadable database of insurers' occupation lists.** The UK industry standard occupation code list is maintained by Criterion (the custodian, working with major insurers). It is not published as open data. A 2016 article put the ABI list at 2,100+ occupations; a 2026 web page claims about 3,500 (unverified).
- **No single shared list.** Each insurer and comparison site shows its own options and rates them differently (Howden).
- **Published averages exist but are not what we need.** Confused.com and GoCompare publish average premiums by job title. They compare different people, so they do not show what one person saves by switching between accurate titles.
- **ONS SOC 2020** (Standard Occupational Classification) is open data. It is not what insurers use, but its 9 major groups are a good privacy-friendly way to ask "what kind of work" without a title. The guided check uses broad groups like these.
- **Authoritative list:** contact Criterion, or check whether a specific insurer publishes its list.

## Plan
1. Today: the guided check asks only a broad work group and tells people to test every title that is genuinely true for them (formal title, industry term, a genuine second job). It never suggests an untrue title.
2. Next: add the broad work group to the optional anonymous report (a new banded field) so the community data can show how much "alternative accurate title" changes quotes, by group, once 20 reports exist. Needs a schema change, privacy-page update and legal sign-off.
3. Later: a curated, hand-written list of common synonym pairs for accurate titles (for example "software developer" and "programmer"), each checked by a person. No scraping.

## Rules
- Never imply a title can be chosen for price alone. Insurers can check and a false title can void a claim.
- Do not publish per-insurer title ratings without legal advice (see question 15 in `legal-review-brief.md`).
